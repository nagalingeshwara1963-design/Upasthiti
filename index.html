Upasthiti User Guide

1. Recommended Classroom Photo Technique: For classes larger than 30 students, capture 2 to 3 photos covering Left, Centre, and Right zones. Good lighting and minimal motion blur significantly enhance detection.

2. Keyboard Navigation in Attendance Review: Use ↑ and ↓ arrows to select students, Space to toggle Present/Absent, and Enter to cycle status.

3. Mobile Smart Capture: Scan the QR code with any smartphone on the network to upload classroom photos directly without cables.
